# Clinica Leen – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Clinica Leen Medical Center is a multi-specialty healthcare provider located in Sharjah, UAE, offering dental care, dermatology, aesthetic treatments, skincare, gynecology, and other wellness services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Clinica Leen as a professional, patient-focused medical centre built around its core message: **Care. Comfort. Confidence.** The design prioritises clear service discovery, stronger medical credibility, and a more accessible appointment enquiry journey.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Hero Section and First Impression
- Replaced the unreliable hero image presentation with a more dependable visual introduction.
- Improved the headline to communicate the clinic's healthcare and aesthetic services more clearly.
- Strengthened the primary appointment call to action.
- Addressed the previous failed hero image and generic messaging that weakened the homepage's first impression.

### 2. Improved Medical Trust and Credibility
- Strengthened the presentation of medical professionals and specialist services.
- Improved the structure of the medical team section to support professional credentials and expertise.
- Focused testimonial and patient feedback presentation on genuine, verifiable information.
- Improved the presentation of insurance information without implying unconfirmed coverage.
- Addressed the previous reliance on initials, promotional testimonials, and insufficiently supported credibility information.

### 3. Clearer Appointment and Service Enquiry Journey
- Improved the presentation and organisation of dental, dermatology, aesthetic, and wellness services.
- Made treatment information easier to explore and understand.
- Increased the visibility of contact details and appointment-related calls to action.
- Improved the appointment enquiry pathway to provide clearer next steps rather than relying solely on WhatsApp redirection.
- Addressed the previous difficulty of understanding available treatments and contacting the clinic.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more professional healthcare-focused visual direction aligned with Clinica Leen's patient-care positioning.
- **Homepage Layout:** Improved content hierarchy to guide visitors from the clinic introduction to medical services, professional credibility, and appointment enquiries.
- **Service Navigation:** Organised treatment categories to make dental, aesthetic, dermatology, and wellness services easier to discover.
- **User Experience:** Improved access to essential clinic information, contact details, and appointment actions.
- **Trust Presentation:** Gave greater importance to medical expertise and genuine patient feedback while avoiding unsupported claims.
- **Brand Consistency:** Reinforced the clinic's Care. Comfort. Confidence. message throughout the homepage experience.

---

**Project Focus:** A stronger healthcare first impression, credible medical presentation, and a clearer patient journey from treatment discovery to appointment enquiry.
